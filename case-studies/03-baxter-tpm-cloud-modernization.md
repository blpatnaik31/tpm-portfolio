# Case Study 3 — Baxter International: Legacy-to-Cloud Modernization of FDA-Regulated Systems

**Role:** Technical Project/Program Manager · **Period:** Apr 2019 – Mar 2021 (2 years) · **Scope:** Global, cross-functional across QA, IT, Regulatory, Supply Chain · **Reported to:** Senior Director, Quality and Regulatory

## Problem

Baxter's product-hold system ran on legacy SharePoint — it couldn't scale to global operations, was causing product release delays, and had limited audit-trail capability at a point where product safety and FDA compliance intersected directly with supply-chain traceability. Recall tracking was still manual, which meant slow decision cycles during time-sensitive recall events and inconsistent, hard-to-audit execution. Underneath both problems was fragmented data across quality and supply-chain systems, driving heavy manual reconciliation and unreliable reporting.

## Stakeholders

QA, IT, Regulatory, and Supply Chain (cross-functional planning to meet FDA standards); global site teams (rollout and training); executive leadership (program lifecycle visibility).

## Approach

- **Baxter Hold System (BHS) cloud migration** — led the migration of BHS from SharePoint to AWS, integrated the new AWS-based system with JDE and broader ERP for end-to-end traceability, and owned the full program lifecycle: scope, architecture planning, stakeholder alignment, testing, go-live.
- **Recall execution automation** — designed and built a cloud-based recall automation system replacing manual tracking, automating recall execution, notification routing, and reporting for FDA audit readiness; validated requirements with Regulatory, QA, and Supply Chain.
- **SQL Server data infrastructure** — designed SQL Server infrastructure to consolidate fragmented data sources, integrated it with JDE and the quality management systems, and migrated legacy data with zero loss.
- **Global training & change management** — built SOPs, training manuals, and walkthroughs for all three new systems, ran live training across global regions, and set up feedback loops to close post-launch issues fast.

Delivery was Agile via JIRA throughout — roadmap definition, backlog management, and adoption tracking across global sites.

## Artifacts produced

Cloud migration architecture and cutover plan, automated recall-reporting workflows, consolidated SQL Server data model, SOPs and training materials, post-launch feedback/issue tracking.

## Outcome

- **30% faster product release cycles** and **100% supply chain visibility** — BHS AWS migration
- **30% faster decision cycles** during recall events — automation replacing manual tracking
- **75% reduction in manual reconciliations** and **zero data loss** — SQL Server consolidation
- **30% reduction in onboarding time** and near-zero post-launch issues — structured global change management

## Why it matters

Four interlocking programs, one team, two years, all under active FDA compliance — this is the strongest evidence I have of taking a legacy, high-risk system to a modern, auditable, cloud-native one without breaking anything the business depended on in the meantime.
