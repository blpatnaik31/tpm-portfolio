# Case Study 1 — Baxter International: Post-Acquisition ERP Integration & Global Quality Governance

**Role:** Senior Technical Project/Program Manager · **Period:** Jul 2022 – Jun 2025 (3 years) · **Scope:** 50+ global manufacturing/quality sites · **Reported to:** Senior Director, Quality and Regulatory

## Problem

Baxter International acquired Hill-Rom in 2022. Hill-Rom's product inventory had no path into Baxter's existing product-hold process, which created a visibility gap in the supply chain and a real risk of product release delays and compliance exposure at the exact moment regulatory scrutiny on the combined entity was highest. Separately, Baxter's quality compliance workflows were inconsistent across regions — audit risk and operational variability in inspection processes — and the List of Values (LoVs) used for reporting across global functions had never been standardized, making cross-functional data analysis unreliable and inflating audit findings.

## Stakeholders

Quality (global, primary delivery partner), Manufacturing Operations (site-level rollout), IT/ERP, Supply Chain, Regulatory/Compliance, and executive/portfolio leadership for dashboard reporting and release decisions.

## Approach

- **Hill-Rom Hold System Integration** — defined integration requirements between Hill-Rom's inventory systems and Baxter's Hold System, orchestrated the system update to support Hill-Rom SKUs, and linked it to JD Edwards (JDE) for end-to-end traceability. Coordinated validation across Quality, Supply Chain, and IT against regulatory requirements.
- **Global QMS rollout** — led rollout of a centralized Quality Management System integrated with JDE, including standardized inspection workflows, coordinated deployment across global manufacturing/quality sites, and ran the stakeholder communication and change management needed to drive adoption.
- **LoV standardization** — consolidated 500+ List of Values across global functions into a single standardized framework, created governance structures defining ownership and change-control processes, and partnered with global function leads to drive adoption of the new model.

Managed the full program lifecycle across all three workstreams: scope definition, milestone tracking (MS Project), resource alignment, and risk mitigation.

## Artifacts produced

Integration requirements documents, milestone/portfolio dashboards (MS Project), QMS inspection workflow standards, LoV governance framework with defined ownership and change-control processes, executive release-update reporting.

## Outcome

- **30% reduction in product release delays** — Hill-Rom Hold System integration with JDE
- **25% improvement in audit readiness** and **30% reduction in manual processes** — QMS rollout
- **40% reduction in audit findings** — LoV standardization across 500+ values
- Led post-acquisition integration of Hill-Rom product lines into Baxter's compliance infrastructure within the first year of acquisition close

## Why it matters

A single role spanning post-M&A integration, quality governance, and ERP-backed transformation — with measurable outcomes on all three — is an uncommon combination. It's the clearest evidence I have of running enterprise-scale programs where regulatory exposure and integration complexity are both live at once.
