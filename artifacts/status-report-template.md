# Weekly Status Report Template

One page, sent every Friday, same format every week so stakeholders can scan it in 60 seconds and executives can trend it over months.

---

**Program:** [Name] · **Week of:** [Date] · **Overall Status:** 🟢 Green / 🟡 Yellow / 🔴 Red

### This week
- [2-3 bullets: what actually got done, not what was planned]

### Next week
- [2-3 bullets: what's committed for next week]

### Metrics
| Metric | Target | Actual | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| [e.g. Data accuracy %] | 98% | 96.2% | ↑ |
| [e.g. Open defects] | <10 | 14 | → |

### Risks & blockers
- [Only what's new or changed since last week — link to the full RAID log, don't restate it]

### Decisions needed
- [Explicit ask, explicit owner, explicit deadline — a status report that buries a decision ask in prose gets ignored]

---

## Why this format

Status color reflects the honest trajectory of the program, not how the week felt — green means "on track for the committed date," not "nothing bad happened." A status report's job is to let a stakeholder who reads nothing else from me all month still know where things stand and what I need from them.
